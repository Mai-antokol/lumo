The problem

AI spend is growing fast. Visibility isn't.

R&D organizations are spending more and more on AI coding tools like Claude, but managers have almost no visibility into that spend. They get a monthly bill — and no way to know which projects the money went to, what it was actually used for, or whether it paid off.

No project breakdown

The bill shows a total. It doesn't show which project, team or ticket the spend actually belongs to.

No work breakdown

You can't tell if the AI was writing new code, debugging, doing a code review — or stuck in a loop, quietly burning tokens on nothing.

No sense of payoff

There's no way to connect what you spent to what you got back — the code, the pull requests, the tickets it actually moved.

What we're building

A project-by-project view of what your AI spend actually buys.

Lumo breaks AI spend down by the type of work it went to, and connects every session to what it produced — the code changes, the pull requests, the related Jira tasks, and more.

How it works

Three steps. Zero extra process.

Lumo runs alongside the tools your team already uses — nobody has to change how they work.

1

Connect

Link Claude Code and Jira in a few minutes. No code changes, no new habits for your team to learn.

2

Match

Every AI session is automatically matched to the ticket it was working on, using timing and context — not manual tagging.

3

See

Cost, time and phase — planning, coding, test, deploy — roll up to the ticket, the project, and the person.

Built on trust

Observability for leads. Never surveillance for engineers.

Lumo monitors AI spend and delivery outcomes — not the people behind them. Every design decision we make keeps that line in mind.

Aggregated by default

Team leads see totals and trends. Individual detail stays with the engineer unless they choose to share it.

No message content, ever

Lumo reads usage metadata — tokens, time, ticket — never what was typed or generated in a session.

Clear retention policy

Raw session data ages out on a fixed schedule. Only the roll-ups — cost, time, phase — persist long-term.

Designed with the people it measures

Every metric was reviewed with engineers before it shipped — not only with the managers reading the dashboard.

Where we're going

We're building the intelligence layer for AI adoption across the organization.

ROI per initiative

The true AI cost of every project, feature and deliverable — so leaders can compare teams, justify budgets, and forecast spend with confidence.

Quality and risk

Trace problems in delivered work back to the AI sessions behind them, and learn which patterns lead to rework.

Adoption & best practices

Find which teams get the most out of AI and why, then turn their habits into playbooks the whole organization can use.

Skills insight

Spot where teams keep relying on AI to cover knowledge gaps, so leaders know where to invest in training.

Our approach

We don't replace or modify the AI tools teams already use. We sit alongside them and turn usage into insight — so R&D leaders can understand, measure, and get the most out of their AI investment.
